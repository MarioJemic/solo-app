Privacy policy

Solo · last updated 12 November 2026

Solo is a desktop application that runs on your own Windows computer. This policy explains what Solo does with your data, in particular with data from your Google account when you connect Gmail.

Who processes your data

Solo runs only on your computer. The developer of Solo runs no server for it and receives none of your data: not your e-mail, not your chats, not your sign-in.

Data from Google

When you connect Gmail, Solo asks Google for these permissions:

  • Read your e-mail (gmail.readonly): to search and read messages when you ask Solo's agents to.
  • Send e-mail on your behalf (gmail.send): only after you approve each e-mail on a card that shows its recipients and text.
  • Your e-mail address (openid, email): to show which account is connected.

Solo reads e-mail only when you ask for it in a chat, and only what the task needs. It does not read your mailbox in the background, does not keep copies of your mailbox, and does not use your e-mail for advertising or to train AI models.

AI models you choose

To answer you, Solo sends your messages, and the parts of e-mails an agent read for your request, to the AI model you chose in Solo. A model that runs on your computer keeps everything on it. A model of an online provider (for example OpenAI, Anthropic or Google) receives that text under the provider's own terms and privacy policy. You choose the models; you can use local models only.

Where it is kept

The access Google gives Solo (tokens) is kept on your computer, encrypted with Windows (DPAPI). Your chats are kept on your computer in Solo's data folder. Nothing is kept anywhere else by Solo.

Limited Use

Solo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • In Solo: Settings → Connected apps → Disconnect removes the access from your computer.
  • In your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions → Solo → Remove access.
  • Your chats and data: Settings → Your data → Reset my data, or delete Solo's folder.
