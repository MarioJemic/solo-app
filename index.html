Solo

A personal assistant that runs on your own Windows computer.

Solo lets you chat with the AI models you choose, and lets its agents work for you with your files, the web and the apps you connect. It runs locally: your chats, settings and keys stay on your computer.

Connected apps

You can connect your own accounts, such as Gmail, in Solo's Settings → Connected apps. You sign in with the service itself (for Gmail, Google's own sign-in page); Solo never sees your password. Solo keeps only an encrypted access on your computer, and you can disconnect at any time.

With Gmail connected, Solo's agents can search and read your e-mail when you ask them to, and send an e-mail only after you approve it on a card that shows the recipients and the text.
